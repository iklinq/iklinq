What exactly is iKlinq?

iKlinq is an ecosystem in development for the cleaning industry. It is designed to bring business management, professional networking, marketplace opportunities, and client connections into one experience.

Who is the platform for?

It is being designed for independent cleaning professionals, growing cleaning businesses and teams, and clients looking for a clearer way to discover and connect with service providers.

What could I manage inside the app?

The product concept includes requests, quotes, schedules, recurring work, team coordination, client history, service activity, professional profiles, and an operating overview.

How are the community and marketplace different?

The community is focused on professional knowledge and relationships. The marketplace is designed around practical opportunities such as jobs, partnerships, suppliers, and industry resources.

Where will iKlinq launch first?

The initial launch is planned for California. Availability in additional regions will be shared as the product develops.

When will early access be available?

iKlinq is still in development. The interest list will open before launch, and this page will be updated when registration becomes available.
