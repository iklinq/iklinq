What to know
before launch.

What is iKlinq?

iKlinq is a platform in development for the cleaning industry, combining business operations, professional networking, a specialized marketplace, and client connections.

Who is it for?

It is being designed for independent cleaning professionals, growing cleaning businesses and teams, and clients seeking a clearer way to find and work with providers.

Which business tasks will it support?

The product direction includes requests, quotes, one-time and recurring schedules, team coordination, client history, service activity, and an operating overview.

What makes the community useful?

It is intended for practical industry conversations, peer knowledge, relevant connections, and professional reputation—not a generic social feed.

What belongs in the marketplace?

The marketplace is planned around service opportunities, suppliers, business partnerships, and resources relevant to cleaning professionals and companies.

Will it work for solo professionals and teams?

That is the goal: a simple operating flow for independent professionals, with team coordination and shared context for businesses as they grow.

Where will iKlinq launch first?

The initial launch is planned for California. Additional regions will be communicated as the product develops.

What happens after I request early access?

Your information is sent to the iKlinq team. You may receive product updates and an invitation when early access becomes available.
